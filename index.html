Privacy Policy

Critter Cascade (Android) · Effective 5 October 2026

The short version: Critter Cascade doesn't ask you to create an account and doesn't collect your name, email or contacts. Your progress and settings stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads (personalised only if you agree, where the law requires consent). If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play handles the payment.

1. Who we are

Critter Cascade is a game published by Tethered Systems ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: tethered.systems@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your phone. We don't receive it.

  • Game progress: levels cleared, medals, best times, coins, attempts.
  • Settings: reduce motion, vibration, game speed, which tips you've seen, and whether all levels are unlocked.
  • Ad settings: when an ad was last shown (so ads aren't shown too often) and whether you own "Remove ads".
  • Device ID for unlock codes: Settings shows an ID derived from your device's Android ID, which you may choose to send us to receive an unlock code. The app itself never transmits it.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it from your phone.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner on the start screen and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate your general location) and device information (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. Without consent you may see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ads > Privacy options. Wherever you live, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only checks with Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can remove ads and restore the purchase on a new phone. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Children

Critter Cascade is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Californian law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we don't hold data about you ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google for ad and purchase data. You can also delete all game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For anything else, email us.

8. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google is kept and protected under Google's policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the game gains new features. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be noted in the app's store listing.
